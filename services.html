Ducklin Group is currently in the process of registering with the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) as a Small UK AIFM. We are not yet authorised or regulated by the FCA. About Our Structure

Small UK Alternative Investment Fund Manager

A Small UK AIFM is a lighter-touch regulatory regime for fund managers operating below defined asset under management thresholds, registered — rather than fully authorised — with the FCA.

Small AIFM Thresholds (UK)

£100m AUM Maximum AUM for leveraged funds to qualify as a Small AIFM under the UK AIFMD regime
£500m AUM Maximum AUM for unleveraged, closed-ended funds with no redemption rights for 5 years
