About Ducklin Group

Two decades of excellence in investment advisory — built on integrity, research, and lasting client relationships.

Our Mission

Preserving & Growing Generational Wealth

Founded in 2006, Ducklin Group was established on a simple but powerful belief: every client deserves an advisor who acts with the same care and conviction they would apply to their own family's wealth.

We have grown from a boutique advisory shop into a full-service investment firm, but our core philosophy has never changed — rigorous fundamentals, transparent communication, and a genuine long-term partnership with every client we serve.

Today, we advise institutions, family offices, and high-net-worth individuals across a broad range of investment needs, from portfolio construction to complex M&A transactions.

"Our goal isn't to be the biggest firm — it's to be the most trusted one."

— Ducklin Group Founding Principle

Milestones That Define Us

Expanded services to include direct private equity investing, targeting growth-stage companies across technology and healthcare sectors.

M&A Advisory Practice Established

Formalized our M&A advisory capabilities, bringing transaction specialists in-house to serve mid-market deal flow.
